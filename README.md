# AI And the Future of US Work
Examination of AIOE, projected job growth , and wage categories in the US (2024-2034)
Advances in artificial intelligence and automation are rapidly changing the labor market and creating widespread uncertainty about future employment opportunities. Workers, educators, employers, and policymakers need reliable information to identify which occupations are projected to grow, which are at greater risk of automation, and where labor shortages or surpluses may occur. Understanding the trends can help individuals make informed decisions.
